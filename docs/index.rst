==========================================================
How to Sign In to Your Ancestry Member Account
==========================================================

Signing in to your Ancestry member account is the gateway to exploring your family history, building your family tree, and accessing your DNA results.

Whether you are a new member or a returning user, understanding the various sign-in methods and troubleshooting common issues ensures a smooth experience. This guide provides comprehensive instructions for signing in to your Ancestry account across different devices, explains the available sign-in options, and addresses common login challenges.

Understanding Your Ancestry Account
===================================

Your Ancestry account remains active even after your membership expires[citation:3]. Once your membership is canceled, you still have an account as a registered guest, giving you access to many free resources on the site[citation:3]. You can continue to sign in to your Ancestry account, view and build your family trees, and view your AncestryDNA results[citation:3].

If you have multiple accounts, each person needs their own email address and account to use Ancestry[citation:3]. Family plan members each have their own separate account with unique usernames, passwords, and privacy settings[citation:8].

Sign-In Methods
===============

Ancestry provides three ways to sign in to your account[citation:1][citation:2]:

Sign in with Email/Username
---------------------------

This is the standard sign-in method using your Ancestry account credentials[citation:1]. To use this method:

- Go to the top-right corner of any Ancestry page and click "Sign in"[citation:1]
- Enter your email address or username and your password[citation:1]
- Click "Sign in"

If you need help with your password, use the "Forgot password" link to reset it[citation:1].

If you have enabled two-step verification, you will be prompted to enter a verification code after your password, then choose whether to remember the device before submitting the code[citation:1][citation:6].

Sign in with Google
-------------------

You can sign in using your Google account credentials[citation:1][citation:2]:

- Click "Sign in with Google"
- Choose an account or select "Use another account"[citation:1]
- Enter your email or phone number and select "Next"
- Enter your Google password and select "Next"
- Complete any verification steps if prompted[citation:1]

Connecting your Ancestry account with Google does not give Google access to your Ancestry account beyond the fact that you are signing in to Ancestry[citation:10].

Sign in with Apple
------------------

Apple users can sign in with their Apple ID[citation:1][citation:2]:

- Click "Sign in with Apple"
- Enter your Apple ID and password
- Enter the Apple verification code if prompted
- Decide whether to trust this browser
- Confirm using your Apple ID with Ancestry by selecting "Continue"[citation:1]

If you choose "Hide my Email" when signing up with Apple, your account will be associated with a privaterelay.appleid.com email address created by Apple[citation:10]. This is an Apple Relay Email, and you will need to access it to receive emails from Ancestry[citation:10].

Signing In on the Mobile App
============================

The Ancestry app provides convenient access from your mobile device. To sign in:

- Open the Ancestry app
- Tap "Sign in"[citation:6][citation:7]
- Sign in with your Ancestry account (username/email and password), Google, or Apple[citation:6][citation:7]
- If prompted, enter the two-step verification code and tap "Submit"[citation:6][citation:7]

**Device Compatibility**

If you see a message that the Ancestry app is not compatible with your device, check for device updates first[citation:6]. If your phone is up to date and you still see the message, the app will not install on that device. Try accessing Ancestry from a recommended browser instead[citation:6].

**Unable to Sign In**

If you can sign in to Ancestry using a browser but not through the app, uninstall and reinstall the Ancestry app, then try to sign in again[citation:6][citation:7]. If you cannot sign in using the app or a browser, reset your password[citation:6][citation:7].

Troubleshooting Common Issues
=============================

**Forgot Password**

If you don't know your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page[citation:1][citation:12]. Enter your email address and follow the instructions to reset your password[citation:12].

**Signing In to the Wrong Account**

If you see an invitation to upgrade when trying to view records, you may be signed in to the wrong account[citation:4]. From any Ancestry page, click your name or username in the top-right corner and select "Account Settings" to see which membership is attached to the account you are signed in to[citation:4].

If you need to switch accounts, sign out first, then sign in with the correct credentials[citation:1].

**Two-Step Verification Issues**

If you are having trouble with two-step verification, you can choose to sign in with Google or Apple instead of using your Ancestry credentials, as the verification code is only required when signing in with your email and password[citation:10].

**Clearing Cache and Cookies**

If you have confirmed you are in the right account but still have issues, try clearing the cache and cookies in your browser, signing in to your account again, and viewing the same content[citation:4]. Also, ensure you are using a supported browser[citation:4].

What Happens After Signing In
=============================

Once signed in, you can:

- **View and build your family trees**: Add people, photos, names, dates, and other facts[citation:3]
- **View AncestryDNA results**: Access your ancestral regions, journeys, matches, and some traits (if you have taken a DNA test)[citation:3]
- **Receive and respond to messages**: Communicate with other Ancestry members[citation:3]
- **See new Ancestry Hints**: Receive hints and view limited details about them[citation:3]
- **Search free databases**: Access free index collections[citation:3]

If you have a membership, you also have access to premium records and features[citation:3][citation:4].

Signing Out of Your Account
===========================

When you are finished with your session, especially on a public computer or mobile device, remember to sign out[citation:1]. To sign out, click your name or username in the top-right corner of any Ancestry page and select "Sign out"[citation:1][citation:12].

Conclusion
==========

Signing in to your Ancestry member account is straightforward when you know the available methods and how to address common issues. Whether you prefer using your email, Google, or Apple, Ancestry provides flexible options to access your family history research. If you encounter difficulties, try clearing your cache, verifying you are in the correct account, or resetting your password. With your account active, you can continue exploring your family story.
